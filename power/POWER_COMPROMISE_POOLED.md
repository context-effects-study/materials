## Reading

After the descriptive cells leave, the pooled compromise test runs on 4,200 compromise choices against 4,200 binary choices, and the relative share then drops the new-extreme choosers. At the first Holm step of the six-effect family its power at a 4-point effect is 0.75 to 0.81 (30 to 15 percent extreme choosers) and at a 5-point effect 0.94 to 0.96. The smallest compromise effect it detects with 0.80 power is 4.0 to 4.2 points at .05/6. The other five non-similarity effects keep power of 0.98 at a 4-point effect at .05/6 (the manuscript's .05/7 gives 0.98).
