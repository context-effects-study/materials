Reading. The registered rule keeps its false alarms at or below the family-wise 5 percent level by construction (it fires only on a Holm rejection), and the simulation confirms the rate at the planned sizes. The old point rule fired on noise about one run in seven. The price of the guarantee is sensitivity on a single effect: a first-trial contamination of 5 points on one effect is caught about three times in ten, because the first-trial arm has 1,000 choices, while contamination of 10 points or more on one effect, or of 5 points shared across the effects, is caught in most runs (see the table). The old rule's apparent sensitivity at 5 points is mostly its false-alarm rate.
