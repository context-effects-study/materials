## The design requirement

Under independent draws at the planned human sizes, a wrong scenario appears in at most 1 of 20 runs per archetype. Reported, not gated: the faithful archetype reaches S1 in 192 of 200 runs and is not resolved with S1 still possible in 2. Over the limit and reported: CONVERGE_OLD wrong in 13 of 200 runs, reported in Web Appendix E. PASS.
