(Generated by resolution_statement.py, 2026-09-29, 200 runs per planted value, the full pipeline with the joint bootstrap at the registered 95 percent level. Planned human sizes: 350 choices per published-tier cell and 700 per invented cell. Model calls: the registered plan, 10 independent draws per text prompt and 60 per picture prompt. A result counts as resolved when it meets the registered resolution rule. Every planted effect is set against the cell's marginal human effect, so the planted differences and ratios are the true ones. The smallest difference reported is the smallest from which every larger grid value also reaches 80 percent; with 200 runs per value, a share near 0.80 has a standard error of about 0.03. Every share counts the runs in which both positive controls held, 161 of the 200 runs of the tests' group; a positive control failed in the others, because on the synthetic lattice the frequency-decoy control has one calibrated premium-target cell, against three in the design.)
