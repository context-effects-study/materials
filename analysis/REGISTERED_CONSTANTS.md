## Rules stated with the constants

- **Human replication criterion.** An effect replicates when its pooled contrast is significant after Holm within its family at 0.05, runs in the predicted (positive) direction, and has a Bayes factor above 3. The Bayes factor compares a half-normal prior on the effect (positive values only, scale 10 points) with no effect, using a normal likelihood built from the pooled estimate and its cluster-robust standard error (Dienes 2014).
- **Positive controls.** The frequency-decoy cells whose target is the premium option (predicted direction, significant at 0.05, Bayes factor above 3) and the compromise effect (its replication criterion). If one fails, that effect's row of the fidelity map and every pattern-test reading that uses the effect (the memorization, argument-strength and trajectory tests, the descriptive format comparison, and that effect's knows-it-versus-shows-it reading) are reported as uninformative about simulation.
- **First-trial check.** Scope: the seven pooled contrasts of the core design. For each, the effect in participants' first choices is compared with the effect in their later choices; the check fires when a difference is significant at family-wise 0.05 (Holm across the seven) and the full-sample and first-trial estimates differ by more than 5 points. Simulated false-alarm rate at the planned sizes: 0.047 (power/FIRST_TRIAL_RULE_SIM.md).
- **Similarity supplement check.** The similarity effect is estimated from core-design choices and from similarity-supplement choices in the same cells, each against the core design's two-option control and with the same cell weights. If the two differ by more than 5 points and the difference is significant at 0.05, the core-design estimate becomes the similarity benchmark.
- **Classes.** HIGH: same direction, 0.7 to 1.5 times the human effect; OVER: same direction and above 1.5 times; PARTIAL: 0.25 to 0.7 times; LOW: below 0.25 times, or opposite in sign by 3 points or less; ANTI: opposite in sign to a replicated human effect by more than 3 points. Human effects under 2 points are not classed.
- **Model estimates.** Each model effect is reported with the number of calls behind it and a percentile bootstrap interval from 2,000 resamples of the calls, drawn within each combination of stimulus, option order and paraphrase (seed 20260929); descriptive, never a test.
- **Resolving the pattern-test readings.** The same 2,000 resamples are drawn together with resamples of the human choices within each cell (each participant contributes one choice to a cell), and every quantity a pattern-test reading uses (a group's folded fidelity, a difference between groups, a difference between two models or two reasoning settings) is recomputed on each resample, with the groups' members and signs fixed by the point estimates. A resample in which a group's human effects sum to zero or less leaves that group undefined and widens the interval at both ends. A reading that a difference exists needs its estimate past the cutoff and its 95 percent interval clear of the no-difference range (memorization, published-values against new-values tier on the same categories: drop above 0.30 and interval above 0.10; argument-strength: each difference above 0.30 and its interval above 0.12; trajectory and reasoning: difference and whole interval beyond 0.15). A reading of no difference needs its estimate inside the no-difference range and its interval short of the cutoff (memorization: interval below 0.30; argument-strength: within 0.30 either way; trajectory: within 0.15). The format test is descriptive (estimate and interval, no reading). Any other result is not resolved and supports no scenario; when the interval rules out every registered reading, the result is described and is still not resolved. The undershoot requirement applies to the memorization sensitivity re-readings too. A scenario that attributes an account (retrieval, reason construction, rational override, the reversed orderings, rising fidelity) is claimed only on its resolved reading; when that reading is open, neither it nor any later scenario is claimed and the model is reported as not resolved, with the scenarios left open, unless every way the reading could resolve leads to the same scenario. High fidelity needs no resolved steep memorization reading, reversed ordering or decline down the ordering, is blocked by an open reading only when that reading's estimate points to an account, and carries the qualifier "pattern tests not resolved at the design's resolution" (naming the open tests) whenever a pattern test is open. The human resample treats each participant's choices in different cells as independent; beside each pooled human effect, the width of its resampled interval is reported as a share of the width of its registered cluster-robust interval.
- **Phantom read as retrieval.** A model phantom effect reads as consistent with retrieval of a described regularity when it is OVER against a replicated human phantom effect, or larger than 3 points in the phantom's predicted direction where the human phantom effect failed the criterion.
- **First-trial floor.** After the first-trial check fires, an effect that replicates but is smaller than 8 points is classed descriptively only; it leaves the model's overall class, the model-level account and every pattern test.
- **Folded fidelity.** A pattern-test group's fidelity is the sum of its model effects, each taken in the direction of the matched human effect, divided by the sum of the human effect sizes; a value above 1 is folded to 2 minus the value, and every value is capped below at -1. A reading that a group falls short also needs its value before folding to be below 1.
- **Format test when a human effect is small.** If the human effect in words or pictures is under 2 points, no fidelity difference is computed for that format (the format test is descriptive in every case).
- **Memorization sensitivity.** The published-values and new-values tiers, each effect on the same categories in both tiers; baselines inside the 25 to 75 percent limits only, then a weighted regression of each category-tier cell's fidelity on the stimulus tier, the cell's baseline distance from 50 percent, and whether the cell's decoys target the premium or the budget option. Both re-readings are resolved against their own 95 percent intervals, as the memorization test is.
- **Phantom equivalence.** The quantity bounded is the pooled human phantom effect. It is judged absent when its 90 percent interval lies inside minus 4 to plus 4 points.
- **Reversed ordering.** When the bottom position (extremity) exceeds both the top position (attraction) and the middle position (compromise) by more than 0.3, the reading is evidence against reason construction; recognition at or above 0.5 on every attraction or compromise effect the model misses attributes it to rational override on choice sets that offer an argument, otherwise no account is attributed.
- **Effect without a human counterpart.** In a cell whose effect failed the human criterion, a model effect larger than 3 points (either sign) is listed per model and effect; it never enters an agreement measure, and ANTI stays reserved for reversals of replicated human effects.
- **S7, Human effects fail.** 4 or more of the six non-similarity effects fail.
- **Model-level account.** Claimed only when 4 or more of the six non-similarity effects that passed the human criterion carry the same per-effect account.
- **Deviation sensitivity set.** backpacks, new values, numbers; earbuds, new values, numbers; restaurants, new values, numbers; standing desks, invented category, numbers; water bottles, new values, pictures; electric kettles, invented category, numbers; water bottles, published values, numbers. The analysis computes all seven pairs from the calibration record: the six adjustable pairs by option identity under the calibration rule (a pair is final when the compromise target's share lies in 40 to 45 percent, or when its 95 percent interval overlaps that band while the share stays between 33 and 50 percent), and the published pairs whose measured share (the point estimate) lies outside the 25 to 75 percent limits, which is only the water-bottles pair at 80.0 percent. It refuses to run when the result differs from this list. The calibration flags are only cross-checked against it; no flag decides anything.
- **Re-running the model calls.** A re-run reproduces a model effect when the deposited estimate lies inside the re-run's 95 percent bootstrap interval over calls.
