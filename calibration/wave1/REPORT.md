# Calibration wave 1: what ran, what it found, what was decided

Wave 1 measured all 25 two-option pairs at their starting values: the 7 published tier-1 pairs
(check-only, never adjusted), the 7 published-category tier-2 pairs, the 3 invented-category
pairs, and the 8 format pairs (4 words, 4 pictures). 400 approved Prolific participants; the
analysis kept 392 (3,920 choices). The mechanical per-pair numbers are in
`../calibration_report.md`; the launch and reconciliation record is in
`../launch_records/WAVE1_LAUNCH_RECORD.md`.

What it found:

- 17 of the 18 adjustable pairs were outside the 40-45 percent target band, almost all with the
  target too popular. The registered mechanical rule (raise the too-popular option's price by 10
  percent of its value per 10 share-points of imbalance) was logged for each and applied in
  wave 2.
- 5 of the 7 published tier-1 pairs were also out of band, measured at 64.6 to 80.0 percent.
  These are reported as facts and never adjusted (their values are fixed by fidelity to the
  published sources); the water-bottles tier-1 pair sat at 80 percent, beyond the registered
  25-75 limit, and is handled under the analysis plan's provision for defective stimuli.
- The pictures rendering itself shifted choices: the hard-drives pair drew 91.9 percent in
  pictures against 69.4 percent in numbers at identical values.
- Two comprehension checks missed the registered 70 percent detection benchmark (frequency-decoy
  61 percent, extreme-option 60 percent), and the range-question answer key was found to predate
  the stimulus rebuild; the corrected key (disclosed, both scorings reported in
  `../RNG_KEY_CORRECTION_2026-07-02.md`) rescores wave 1 at 51 percent.
- The registered realism pre-check for the three invented categories had not run in this
  instrument; the deviation is recorded, and the check rode with waves 2 and 3 instead.

Decisions taken to wave 2 (all recorded as dated amendments in `../CALIBRATION_DESIGN.md`):
shared values across a category's formats, the corrected range key riding unchanged as a
re-check, clarified wording on the two missing checks, and the realism block added.
