# Calibration wave 1: what ran, what it found and what we decided

Wave 1 measured all 25 two-option pairs at their starting values: the 7 pairs with published values (tier 1, which
we measure but never adjust), the 7 pairs with new values in the published categories (tier 2), the 3 pairs of the
invented categories, and the 8 pairs of the format module (4 in words and 4 in pictures). Prolific approved 400
participants, and the analysis kept 392 of them (3,920 choices). The original report
(`results/calibration_report_wave1.md`) gives the numbers of each pair, and `README.md` gives the record of the
study and accounts for every row of the export.

## What we found

- Seventeen of the eighteen adjustable pairs were outside the target band of 40 to 45 percent, most of them above
  it. For each of them, we logged the adjustment that the pre-registered rule gives: change the price of the option
  chosen too often by 10 percent of its value for every 10 points of imbalance. In wave 2, nine pairs received this
  adjustment, and the eight pairs of the format module took the values of their category's numbers pair.
- Five of the seven pairs with published values were also outside the band, at shares of 64.6 to 80.0 percent. We
  report these shares and never adjust these pairs, because their values must stay as published. The pair of water
  bottles, at 80.0 percent, lies outside the limits of 25 and 75 percent. It stays in the design with its measured
  baseline, and the pre-registered sensitivity analysis leaves it out.
- The pictures changed the choices. At identical values, 91.9 percent of respondents chose the target among the
  hard drives shown as pictures, against 69.4 percent among the hard drives shown as numbers.
- Two comprehension questions missed the benchmark of 70 percent correct: the question about the frequency decoy
  (61 percent) and the question about the new extreme option of the compromise set (60 percent). We also found that
  the answer key of the range question had been written before we rebuilt the range decoy. Scored with the
  corrected key, wave 1 gives 51 percent (`../RNG_KEY_CORRECTION_2026-07-02.md` reports both scorings).
- The planned realism check of the three invented categories was not part of this survey. We report this change of
  order, and waves 2 and 3 measured realism.

## What we decided for wave 2

`../CALIBRATION_DESIGN.md` records each decision with its date. The words and pictures pairs of a category show
the values of its numbers pair. Wave 2 asks the range question again, unchanged, with the corrected key. The two
questions that missed the benchmark are reworded, and wave 2 adds the realism questions.
