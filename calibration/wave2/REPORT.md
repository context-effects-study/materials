# Calibration wave 2: what ran, what it found and what we decided

Wave 2 measured again the 17 pairs that still needed adjustment, at the values adjusted after wave 1, with each
category's formats showing the same values. It also asked the realism questions and the corrected and reworded
comprehension questions. Prolific approved 312 participants, and the analysis kept 309 of them (3,090 choices). The
original report (`results/calibration_report_wave2.md`) gives the numbers of each pair, and `README.md` gives the
record of the study and accounts for every row of the export.

## What we found

- Only 1 of the 17 pairs fell in the band: the robot vacuums, at 42.1 percent, whose values became final.
- On four pairs shown as numbers (Bluetooth speakers, earbuds, hard drives and televisions), we had raised the
  price of the premium option, and its share rose or stayed the same. A second adjustment of the price therefore had
  no support in the data for these pairs. One possible reason is that on sparse pages with two lines of text,
  respondents read the price partly as a sign of quality.
- All three invented categories scored just under the realism threshold of 5.0 (means of 4.53 to 4.77, with 309
  respondents). The comments named the sparse pages with two attributes and some unusual units, and they did not
  name the invented products themselves.
- Three comprehension questions passed again. The frequency question fell to 44 percent correct after its
  rewording, which triggered the rule, stated in advance, of one strengthening of the stimuli. The other questions
  had about 44 respondents each, too few to decide whether a rate near 70 percent passes.

## What we decided for wave 3

We wrote every decision into `../CALIBRATION_DESIGN.md` before the wave-3 data existed:

- set the price from the two measured points where the two waves gave a usable response of the share to the price;
- change a quality attribute by one cautious, realistic step where the change of price had failed;
- allow different values for each format where the same values could not put all of a category's formats in the
  band;
- give every product page a short, realistic description, the planned remedy for realism, and check realism again;
- strengthen the check stimuli of the frequency and range decoys once, as the rule stated in advance allowed;
- give each comprehension structure about 130 respondents;
- fix the decision rule of the final wave in advance: a share in the band, or an interval that overlaps the band with
  a share between 33 and 50 percent.
