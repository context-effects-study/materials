# Calibration wave 2: what ran, what it found, what was decided

Wave 2 re-measured the 17 pairs still needing adjustment, at the wave-1 nudged values shared
across each category's formats, and added the realism block and the corrected or clarified
comprehension items. 312 approved Prolific participants; the analysis kept 309 (3,090 choices).
The mechanical per-pair numbers are in `../calibration_report_wave2.md`; the launch and
reconciliation record is in `../launch_records/WAVE2_LAUNCH_RECORD.md`.

What it found:

- Only 1 of 17 pairs landed in the band (robot vacuums, 42.1 percent, which became final).
- On four pairs (bluetooth speakers, earbuds, hard drives, televisions) raising the premium
  option's price moved the target's share the WRONG way or not at all, so a second mechanical
  price adjustment had no empirical basis there. On sparse two-line pages, price appears to
  stand in for quality.
- All three invented categories scored just under the 5.0 realism threshold (4.53 to 4.77 at
  n=309), with comments blaming the sparse two-attribute page format and odd units rather than
  the invented products themselves.
- Comprehension checks: three passed again; the frequency check fell to 44 percent after the
  clarification (triggering the pre-stated one-time strengthening rule), and the others sat on
  samples of about 44 per item, too small to be decisive.

Decisions taken to wave 3 (all recorded as dated amendments BEFORE the wave-3 data existed):
set prices by the two-point solve where the two waves gave a usable price response; move a
QUALITY attribute one conservative, market-realistic step where price had failed; allow per-pair
values where shared values could not put all of a category's formats in band; enrich every
product page into a short realistic blurb (the registered realism remedy) and re-check realism;
strengthen the frequency and range check stimuli once, as the pre-stated rule allowed; give
every comprehension structure about 130 respondents; and fix the terminal decision rule in
advance (band, or interval overlapping the band with the share between 33 and 50 percent).
