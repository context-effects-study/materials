# Calibration wave 3 (terminal): what ran, what it found, how everything ended

Wave 3, the terminal wave, re-measured the 16 remaining pairs at per-pair adjusted values on
enriched product pages, with two comprehension items per respondent (about 130 per structure),
the picture-comprehension item, and the realism re-check. 462 approved Prolific participants;
the analysis kept 459 (4,131 choices). The mechanical per-pair numbers and terminal verdicts are
in `../calibration_report_wave3.md`; the launch and reconciliation record is in
`../launch_records/WAVE3_LAUNCH_RECORD.md`. The terminal decision rule had been fixed before the
data were collected.

How the 25 pairs ended (the full list with values is `../FINAL_LOCK_LIST_2026-07-02.json`):

  water bottles 44.1 in band; kettles 44.7; televisions 49.4 by the interval route), robot
  vacuums (wave 2, 42.1), water bottles numbers (wave 1, 41.7), bluetooth speakers 35.9, hard
  drives 47.0 and televisions 49.0 in numbers, kettles 44.7, and bluetooth speakers 37.3 and
  televisions 38.7 in pictures.
- **7 published tier-1 pairs checked and reported, never altered**; one (water bottles, 80.0
  percent) beyond the 25-75 limit and handled under the analysis plan's stimulus-defect
  provision.
- **5 pairs proceed with the deviation documented**: backpacks (28.5) and restaurants (31.2)

- **1 pair removed**: pictures hard drives stayed beyond the 75 percent limit in all three waves

  arithmetic after the removal: 93,100 confirmatory choices across 123 cells.

Comprehension checks across the waves: phantom 87, extreme 88, symmetry 71, and menu 93 percent
pass the 70 percent benchmark; the frequency (34), range (46), and near-copy (33) checks fail
it, with the construct-validity facts reported (the modal frequency-check answer is impossible
under the item's own definition, which points at the item, not the stimulus). The registered
protection stands: the confirmatory human replication arbitrates every benchmark. The
picture-comprehension item came back at 92 percent, which pins the pictures distortions on the
rendering itself, not on misreading.

Realism: the invented categories scored 4.36 / 4.53 / 4.68 at n=459, below the 5.0 threshold
again, which triggered the realism comparison study (`REALISM_COMPARISON.md`): the invented
pages proved as realistic as the published ones on the identical question.
